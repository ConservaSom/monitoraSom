## Key captured facts
- The original normalizes **per-pair**: `score = 1 - dist / max(dist)`, so the
  maximum is pinned to **1 minus the pair's own minimum-relative** and the peak
  sits at the same frame as cor (t075 0.516 @ 10.335 s; t092 0.697 @ 37.345 s).
- `score_min = 0` exactly (the window of maximum distance maps to 0).
- Localization is correct (peak inside the truth window) — dtw was "less broken
  than feared" in the Stage-2 evaluation; the defect is the **scale**, not the
  localization.

## Intentional divergence (refactor CHANGES this — assert the property)
- **RMD-02 — per-pair `1 - dist/max(dist)` scale.** Because the denominator is
  **this pair's own maximum distance**, the score is rescaled independently for
  every (soundscape, template) pair → **not comparable across soundscapes** (a
  weak match against a quiet soundscape can score as high as a strong match
  against a busy one). The refactor replaces it with an **absolute**,
  template-size-normalized score: mean per-cell L1 cost `dist / (sw * bins)`
  mapped through `1 / (1 + cost)` to a bounded `(0, 1]` scale that is
  **pair-independent** (the denominator is the template/window size, not the
  pair's own distances). The refactored dtw series therefore does **not** equal
  this golden; the test asserts that the **source** soundscape scores higher than
  a **non-source** soundscape on the shared absolute scale (the property the
  original scale could not provide).
